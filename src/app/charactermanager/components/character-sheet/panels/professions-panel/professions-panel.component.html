Gathering in Azeroth

For journey gathering, make a Wisdom (Survival) check once per journey stage, or once per day if stages are shorter than a day. The DM sets the DC using the region’s level.

Failure finds nothing. Success finds one resource per day of the stage. Beating the DC by 5 or more makes one of those resources rare. The DM chooses the resources and their GP values. Archaeology, fishing, and skinning have the special rules listed in their entries.

Resources are measured in GP value and can be sold or combined with money for crafting. Specific ingredients and recipes are optional and chosen by the DM.

Your Proficiency Bonus measures profession skill: +2 is apprentice, +3 journeyman, +5 artisan, and +6 master. If your campaign uses training to gain levels, improving each profession takes days equal to your new bonus, at 2 GP per day. At +3, that is 3 days and 6 GP per profession.

Region DCs, resource values, dig-site origins, and magic-item rarity are set with your DM using the source tables.
